What this means: This accuracy rate was measured honestly -- the models were tested against real survey responses they were NOT trained on (a "held-out" set), repeated 10 times with different random splits to avoid relying on a single lucky result. It measures how close the predicted headcount lands to the actual observed headcount in each test, not whether every individual student's prediction was correct. Individual-level accuracy is naturally lower (see model details below) since predicting one specific person's decision is harder than predicting an aggregate trend across many people -- small errors in each direction tend to cancel out when summed, which is why the aggregate headcount accuracy is high even though no single prediction is guaranteed correct.
