Expected Undecided Students: {{ expected_undecided }}

These students are predicted to pursue college but have not selected a specific preferred degree program. They are not included when estimating sections for individual degree programs.

What this means: This breaks the expected headcount down by which specific degree program each student is most likely to choose. "Estimated sections needed" divides the expected student count by {{ STUDENTS_PER_SECTION|default:"40" }} students per section. This overall accuracy figure measures whether the TOTAL predicted mix of students across all programs matches what actually happened, tested on held-out data the models never trained on -- it is a different (and higher) number than each model's raw individual classification accuracy (~50% for specific degree program), because it asks a different question. Predicting the total distribution across many students is a smoother, more forgiving task than predicting any one student's exact choice: a student mispredicted as choosing BSED instead of BIT still contributes correctly to the "roughly N students will choose something technical" pattern in the aggregate. This is analogous to how weather forecasters can accurately predict "30% chance of rain this month" even though they can't say which specific day it will rain.
